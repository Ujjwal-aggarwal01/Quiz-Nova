About QuizNova

QuizNova is an online quiz platform designed to make learning simple, interactive, and enjoyable.

🎯 Our Goal

Our goal is to help students practice their knowledge, improve their skills, and learn through interactive quizzes.

📚 Quiz Categories

QuizNova currently provides quizzes in Mathematics, Programming, Data Structures, and General Knowledge.

⚡ Features

Timed quizzes, instant results, score tracking, multiple categories, and a leaderboard make learning more engaging.

💻 Technology

QuizNova is developed using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and JSON-based question data.
